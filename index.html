How it works

A counter sits in the middle and the game names a target. Exactly one disc on the ring gets you there.

12 48

What you have, what you want

The solid number is your counter. The dashed one is where the round ends.

+7 ×3 ÷2 −4

Add, subtract, multiply, divide

Each disc carries its number; its colour carries the operation. Multiply and divide are physically larger, so you find them without reading.

The ring is the clock

It drains while you think, and every right answer buys some of it back. A good streak is what keeps a run alive.
